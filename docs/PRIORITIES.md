# PlayerPath — Priorities & Roadmap

**Last verified:** 2026-08-01 (statuses checked against current `main`)

The prioritization lens: **for a shipped app with paying users, trust and money come before growth, growth comes before polish, and polish comes before future bets.** Re-verify statuses against code before acting — they drift as work lands outside tracked sessions.

## The next needle mover

**The recruiting profile** (public, video-first athlete page — `docs/RECRUITING_PROFILE_PLAN.md`).

> **Status 2026-08-01: SHIPPED to prod, pending device QA.** The rest of this section is the *why* — it still holds, and it's the yardstick for whether the bet paid. What it no longer describes is work to be done; see #6.

It's the only item that *moves* the growth curve rather than protecting it. It changes *why* people use the app — from "track my kid's stats" (a retention product that competes on features against GameChanger) to "help my kid get seen" (the highest willingness-to-pay motivation in youth sports). It hits all three levers at once:

- **Acquisition** — every profile is a public link athletes share with college coaches, on social, in family group chats. Each share carries PlayerPath branding to exactly the right audience. A referral loop that doesn't exist today; your clips are currently trapped inside the app.
- **Monetization** — justifies a premium tier or its own SKU; the clearest "worth paying for" feature you could ship, because the value is external and legible.
- **Retention** — a *live* public profile gives a reason to keep capturing (kept current like a LinkedIn).

**Cost (honest):** biggest lift on the list — it implies a **web surface** (public read access, hosting, privacy/unpublish controls, PII discipline), not another in-app screen.

**Smallest version that still moves the needle:** one public link per person-group, hero highlight reel up top, thin stat band (de-emphasized for baseball, full band for golf), grad year / position / contact, opt-in publish toggle with an unpublish kill switch. The PlayerPath watermark on that page *is* the growth engine. Ship that, get five real athletes to share their link, watch for click-throughs. That's the signal.

11. **iPad coach tooling** — sidebar → split comparison → filmstrip → slow-mo. Valuable for the segment that now pays per seat, smaller audience than athletes.
12. **Live Activities + notification toggles** — engagement surface, not load-bearing.
13. **Recruiting "done recruiting" closed state** — designed 2026-07-26, not started. `unpublish`'s 404 says *"unpublished or the link is incorrect"* — right for a leaked link, wrong for an athlete who committed, so every coach holding the link gets a message implying the athlete's link is broken. Fix is a `closedAt` field + a 410 closed page served **before** the Pro check (done-recruiting and cancelled-Pro are the same week). No rules change needed. Server half designed and approved; client half sketched only. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-26-recruiting-closed-state-design.md`.

14. **Recruiting: link external scouting profiles** — designed-in-chat 2026-09-06, not started. The recruiting page's weakness is that everything on it is *self-attested* (`showMeasurables` = athlete-typed 60 time / exit velo / throwing velo). Linking out to PBR / Perfect Game / NCSA / FieldLevel (ball) and Junior Golf Scoreboard / AJGA / Clippd (golf) borrows third-party verification and makes PlayerPath the **hub** — the one QR/email link that routes a coach to the verified numbers *and* to the tagged game film nobody else has. Golf is the stronger case: college coaches screen on JGS rank almost mechanically. Constraints that shape the build: **(a)** curated platform picker with per-platform host validation, ~4–5 max — never a free-form URL list, which rots into a junk drawer; **(b)** social handles (X, Hudl) are a *separate* feature — an X handle is a direct contact channel to a minor and must ride the existing per-field opt-in + `contactPublishingBlocked` + `gradYearImpliesUnder13` gates and count toward `hasPublicReplyChannel`; a PBR org page is not the same risk class; **(c)** never render a "Verified ✓" badge — ownership is unverifiable; **(d)** the CF must sanitize the URL itself (https-only, escaped `href`, `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`) — the "display strings built client-side" invariant covers text, not an attribute fed by user-authored Firestore data. **Explicitly out of scope:** scraping/importing stats from those sites (ToS + fragile) and auto-verification. Honest value: cheap polish/credibility on a surface that is still `RecruitingFeature.isEnabled = false` — it does not sell a subscription. **Cheaper adjacent win, do first:** a share-tools nudge to "copy your PlayerPath link for your PBR bio / X bio / email signature", which pushes traffic *in* instead of sending coaches out.

## Tier 5 — Future bets & big refactors (important eventually, not urgent)

15. **Golf sport-abstraction refactor (~262 sites)** — real tech debt that slows every future sport feature, but invisible to users. Do it when it actively blocks you.
16. **Family/Fan viewer role** — explored in chat 2026-09-26, not started. **Recommended promotion to Tier 3 (Trey's call, pending)**: churn is the one number Pricing V2 admits it doesn't model, and this is the most direct lever on it.
    - **The thesis is retention of the *parent*, not acquisition.** Grandparents won't pay and rarely bring new families. Their value is being the audience: "grandma is waiting to see today's game" rewards the parent the same night, not someday. The habit to build is **"every game,"** not "every day" (no streaks — rainouts would punish).
    - **Settled:** athletes film inside PlayerPath (so clips exist at the moment of capture). **Every clip goes to the feed automatically — zero curation work for the parent.** Grandparents never pay. iOS-only for now; Android/web is too big a play to take on today.
    - **Shape:** "following" is a *relationship* any account can hold (an aunt who is also a parent, a coach following a niece), not a third role. A follow-only user gets a simple viewer home. Reactions only in v1 (❤️ back to the parent = the reward loop); no comments. Games only by default; practices/lesson footage and coach notes stay out. Easy per-clip **Hide** in the clip's own menu, since with auto-share, hiding is always after the fact.
    - **Feed ≠ notifications.** Feed = everything. Push = restrained, automatic, no parent work: "game started" (on Start Game), batched "4 new clips" at most every 20–30 min, an immediate push only for a *tagged* hit (bonus, never required), and "Final — went 2-for-3." One push per clip = grandma mutes by weekend two, and she won't open the app without push. **Side effect worth having:** tags become grandma's captions ("Double to left, 2 RBI"), so tagging stops feeling like data entry.
    - 🚨 **Blocker for "live": uploads are Wi-Fi-only by default.** `allowCellularUploads` is true only when `autoUploadMode == .always` (`UserPreferences.swift`), enforced at `UploadQueueManager.swift:682`. Default parents' game clips sit on the phone until home Wi-Fi, so grandma gets everything at 9:40pm. Flipping the default isn't the answer (full-quality clips over cellular = data plan + slow at the field).
    - **Proposed fix — a small family copy per clip:** a small H.264 copy uploads immediately over cellular (a few MB); the full-quality master still waits for Wi-Fi and keeps serving stats/reels/coaches/recruiting. Gets live working without parent effort, and incidentally keeps the Android/web door open (H.264 plays everywhere — same reason `RecruitingWebRenditionService` exists). Costs: on-device conversion battery/time at the field, extra storage (**must not count against the parent's quota** — this feature must never trigger a storage paywall), and a second upload path to maintain. **Open decision:** build it (live product) or skip it (nightly-highlights product)?
    - **Risks:** (a) the real competitor is the family *group text*, which is a conversation, not just file delivery — complement it with a one-tap "Share today's game" recap into the thread rather than trying to replace it; (b) grandparent onboarding (install → sign in → allow notifications) is the real drop-off, more than Android — design for the parent setting it up in person (QR from the parent's phone), and treat notification permission as make-or-break; (c) chicken-and-egg — prompt "Want Grandma to see this?" **right after the parent's first game with clips**, never into an empty feed; (d) family edge cases: teens who don't want their strikeouts shared, divorced/split families and revocation, other kids' faces; (e) minors' privacy — parent-granted, revocable, rules-tested like coach access, and it sharpens the unresolved signup/TOS/privacy-policy age mismatch; (f) cloud bandwidth (egress) grows with every follower — add to the Pricing V2 cost math.
    - **Validate before building:** ask current parents who they text clips to after games — a fast named answer = real demand. **Measure after:** the *same* parent's posting before vs after their first follower joins (comparing parents with vs without followers is selection bias — inviters are already the engaged ones).
